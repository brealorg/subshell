# What subshell does

Everything below is in the app today (1.0.0-rc.2). Where a feature is off until you
turn it on, it says so. Settings paths are written as Settings › Area › Setting.

subshell is a beta. You need your own Reddit Client ID once; subshell ships none
([how](https://brealorg.github.io/subshell/get-started/)). Android 8.0 or newer.

## What sets subshell apart

- **It keeps your place.** Feeds stay where you were when you open a post and go back.
  Optionally they also reopen at your place after the app was closed, either as you left
  them or by loading new posts and finding the spot within four pages
  (Settings › Feeds & content › Resume where you left off, off by default). Pages load as
  you scroll; there are no "load more" buttons.
- **Threads built for reading.** Replies load by themselves. Find in thread searches the
  comments, can step through only the post author's comments (the OP chip) and opens
  collapsed branches for you. Comments new since your last visit are highlighted and the
  header counts them. Thread colours give each depth its own colour (off by default). A
  button jumps between top-level comments (off by default). Reddit's suggested sort is used
  automatically.
- **Moderator tools on the phone.** Per community: the queue, Removed, Edited and
  Unmoderated lists, the mod log, user management (ban, mute, approve), the moderator
  team and permissions. Every post and comment has one **Moderate** entry: approve,
  remove (with the community's removal reasons and an optional message to the author),
  spam, change flair, lock, distinguish, reports. Saved responses, Modmail with internal
  notes and a cross-community queue in the Inbox.
- **Keep the noise out.** Content filters hide posts by word, community, user, domain or
  flair, everywhere or in one community, and nothing changes on Reddit. Mute a community
  for an hour, a day, a week, until a date or for good. Hide a joined community without
  leaving it. An optional language filter hides search results and discovery in languages
  you did not pick, decided on the device (Android 10+, off by default).
- **Know what is new.** Watch up to ten communities and get a notification for each new
  post, and optionally each new comment. Choose which Inbox notifications you want:
  replies, mentions, private messages, Reddit notices, Modmail. Messages arrive as
  conversations and can be answered from the notification.
- **Read offline.** Save the top 50 posts of your feed with their comment threads and
  images (not video) for reading without a connection.
- **Private by design.** No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting. The app contains no
  Firebase or Google Play services. Your sign-in and Client ID are stored encrypted on the
  device. An optional app lock and a requirement to unlock before hidden communities show.
  When the app lock is on, notifications can hide their content. Android's own cloud
  backup is switched off.
- **Your data is yours.** Back up your settings to a folder you choose. Hidden and muted
  communities, post drafts, saved moderator responses and private notes are only included
  if you say so. Export your Reddit data (saved, votes, posts, comments, communities,
  messages and more) as JSON and CSV in one ZIP.
- **Tools for writing.** Drafts for posts and replies. The post composer checks the
  community's posting requirements before you send. Typing `@` or `u/` suggests people from
  the thread you are in. Formatting toolbar with preview. Crosspost. Saved searches
  (off by default). Private notes and colour labels on usernames, never sent to Reddit.
- **Open source and self-updating.** GPL-3.0-or-later. Update from Settings › Updates: the
  APK is checked against a published SHA-256 before Android is asked to install it.

## Reading

- Home, Popular and All; custom feeds you can create, edit and favourite; favourites,
  recent and joined communities; hide a community from the joined list.
- Four layouts: Large, Compact, List (thumbnail on either side) and Grid.
- Sort by Best, Hot, New, Rising, Top and Controversial, with a time range where Reddit
  supports it. Community sorting is remembered per community.
- Unread markers on posts you have not opened. Hide read posts, or reset what counts as
  read.
- Pinned posts as cards or as a compact row. A row that says how many posts your filters
  hid, with a way to show them (off by default).
- A tap on a post's flair opens a search for that flair in the community, newest first.
- Swipe actions on posts and comments: upvote, downvote, save, hide or nothing, per
  direction (off by default).
- Save, hide, share, report, follow replies to a post. Edit, manage (flair, NSFW, spoiler)
  and delete your own posts; edit and delete your own comments.
- Community pages with About, rules, moderators and a read-only wiki. Join, favourite,
  choose your own flair. Opt in to quarantined communities.
- Random NSFW community, with the placement you choose (off by default).
- Profiles with posts and comments, block and unblock, and your own library: saved, upvoted,
  downvoted, hidden, drafts.
- Your reading: counts of opens and time per community, kept on the device only, with
  suggestions to hide or leave communities you never open (off by default).
- Links to reddit.com and redd.it open in the app: posts, comments, communities, users,
  searches, wiki pages and custom feeds.

## Search

- Posts, communities and people from one field. All of Reddit or just the current
  community. Sort and time range. Recent searches. Paste a Reddit link to open it.
- Saved searches: up to 50 per account, included in backups.

## Photos and video

- Galleries, a full-screen viewer with zoom, share and save to Downloads, and a captions
  choice (overlay, below or hidden).
- Reddit video with a quality ceiling per network (High on Wi-Fi and Medium on mobile data
  by default), subtitles and audio language. Videos start muted and autoplay as you
  choose: Always, Wi-Fi only or Never.
- Optional (all off by default): playback speed, double-tap to skip, hold for 2x speed,
  pinch to zoom, picture-in-picture, rotate for wide videos, technical details.
- Landscape full screen: swipe the left half for brightness and the right half for volume
  (on by default).
- Sensitive media is blurred by default; you can load on tap or hide it. NSFW media only
  plays automatically if you turn that on.
- RedGIFs clips in NSFW posts play in the app. Other external video sites (YouTube,
  Streamable and similar) open as links.
- Video and image caches with limits you set (video cache 256 MB to 2 GB).

## Messages and notifications

- Inbox with Activity, Messages and (for moderators) Mod. Mark read, dismiss, select.
- Private messages and Reddit Direct Chat: text, images and GIFs, reactions, typing
  indicator, delete your own messages.
- Several accounts at once, switched from the bottom bar. Everything account-specific is
  kept per account.
- Optionally hide your online status from other users.

## On your device

- Widgets for the Inbox and for posts from Home or a community, which read stored data
  only.
- Launcher shortcuts and "Add to home screen" for communities (off by default).
- Share into subshell from other apps (off by default).
- Tablets and foldables: navigation rail, list and detail side by side, tabletop posture
  for video.
- Predictive back; optional "press Back twice to exit".

## Make it yours

- Light, dark or system; dynamic colour on Android 12+; pure black; UI size 85 to 110 %
  and text size 85 to 130 %; three code fonts; two vote styles and vote colours.
- Left- or right-handed controls and side menu; top and bottom bars that hide on scroll;
  navigation labels; communities written r/name, /r/name or name.
- Comment time as relative, 24-hour or 12-hour; where comment actions sit; whether a tap
  collapses a comment or only its header; collapse long post text; author avatars.
- Reduce motion, TalkBack actions on feed cards, 48 dp touch targets, and bar hiding that
  follows Android's "time to take action".
- Settings are searchable and grouped in ten areas.

## What subshell does not do

So nobody has to find out the hard way:

- No video download. You can save images and export a video to Downloads/subshell.
- No alt text for images, neither viewing nor writing.
- No voting in polls (results show; voting opens Reddit) and no wiki editing.
- No background audio.
- The offline cache of posts is not encrypted by the app; only your sign-in and Client ID
  are.

FAQ

Short answers to what people ask most.

Is subshell made by Reddit?

No. subshell is an independent Android client for Reddit. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Reddit.

Why do I need my own Client ID?

Reddit requires every app that signs you in to identify itself with a Client ID. subshell does not ship a shared one; you create one once through RedReader's sign-in. See the steps.

How is my sign-in stored?

You sign in on Reddit's own page. The resulting session is stored in app-private storage encrypted with the Android Keystore. It is never part of a settings backup, and logging out removes it.

Where do I get it, and how do updates work?

During the beta subshell is a signed APK on GitHub Releases. The app checks for a newer beta about twice a day, shows a notification and the release notes, and opens the release page. It never installs anything by itself.

Does subshell collect data about me?

No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking. The app talks to Reddit on your behalf. Diagnostics are kept on the device and are only shared if you choose to send a report. Details are in the privacy notice.

What about NSFW content?

NSFW posts are hidden until you turn on Show NSFW posts, and sensitive previews are blurred until you tap them. Your Reddit account must also allow mature content. RedGIFs clips play in the app only while NSFW posts are shown.

Does it have notifications?

Yes. While you are signed in, subshell checks Reddit about every 15 minutes and notifies you about replies, mentions, messages, Reddit notices and modmail. Each type can be turned off, and Android 13 or newer asks for permission first.

Can I use several accounts?

Yes. Add accounts in Settings and switch from the account menu. Votes, lists, scroll positions and notifications stay with their own account.

What is in a backup?

Settings, joined communities, favorites and saved searches, written to a folder you choose, with an automatic history and safety snapshots before a restore. Hidden and muted communities, post drafts, saved moderator responses and private user notes are opt-in. Your sign-in, Client ID and app lock are never included.

Does it work on tablets and foldables?

Yes. Wide windows get a navigation rail, a list next to the open post or conversation, a comfortable reading width and Settings as list and detail.

Is there Direct Chat and modmail?

Yes. The Inbox has activity, messages, Reddit's Direct Chat (with reactions and images) and, for moderators, the mod queue and modmail.

Where is the source code?

In brealorg/subshell-source-public, under the GNU GPL v3.0 or later. It gets one snapshot per release, so the source always matches a published APK. That repository is read-only: bugs and ideas go to the main repository, which holds releases, documentation, the website and the issue tracker.

How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

Use the forms on the Support page. Settings › Advanced › Diagnostics can create a privacy-safe report to attach.

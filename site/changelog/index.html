Release notes

What's new

Every public beta, newest first. The same notes are in the app under What's new.

0.84-beta

21 October 2026 · Release on GitHub

New-post notifications, text selection and one sign-in for everything

  • Get a notification when a community you choose has a new post: "Notify about new posts" in the community's ⋮ menu (up to 10 communities).
  • Long press to select and copy text in posts, comments and messages, with Android's Copy, Share and Translate.
  • Sign-in asks for every permission once. Modmail, mod log and the wiki no longer ask again (accounts signed in before approve one more time).
  • If sign-in stops working because Reddit or RedReader changed something, you can edit the redirect URI and user agent yourself, in Settings or during setup.
  • Short confirmations such as "Muted r/x" now disappear by themselves.

0.83-beta

19 October 2026 · Release on GitHub

Choose how posts look, pinned posts first, and pinch to zoom videos

  • Choose how posts look: Large, Compact, List or Grid, from the view button beside the feed (and in Settings).
  • A community's pinned posts now come first in every sort, as cards or as compact highlights.
  • Pinch to zoom videos in the full-screen player (turn it on in Settings).
  • A fresh look: Google's Material Symbols icons, Material 3 Expressive motion, and dynamic colour on by default.
  • More reliable: one unreadable post no longer breaks a whole feed, removed images say so instead of retrying, and the inbox sync retries on a shaky network.

0.82-beta

16 October 2026 · Release on GitHub

A new comment composer, a smaller app and open source

  • Replying is rebuilt: a quick reply card sits on the keyboard, and Expand opens a full-screen editor with formatting and a preview of your comment.
  • subshell is about two-thirds smaller (7.5 MB instead of 24.6 MB) and starts more evenly, thanks to code shrinking and a startup profile.
  • If subshell closes unexpectedly, the next start offers to send a diagnostic report. Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to send it.
  • subshell's source code is now public under GPL-3.0, one snapshot per release, at github.com/brealorg/subshell-source-public.
  • Updated to the newest Material 3 components.

0.81-beta

15 October 2026 · Release on GitHub

Feed videos keep their shape

  • Fixed: tall and very wide videos in the feed were stretched and cut off at the edge while playing.
  • Mention people in comments: type @ or u/ for people from the thread, then Reddit's people search.
  • Subshell checks for updates when you come back to it, so a new release shows up within the hour.
  • The update dialog can turn on update notifications if Android has them switched off.

0.80-beta

15 October 2026 · Release on GitHub

Material 3 throughout, a new video player and better accessibility

  • Material 3 throughout: one top bar per screen, a navigation rail on tablets and foldables, list rows and segmented choices in Settings.
  • Video: a new seek bar with live frames, playback options in post and full screen, speed, double-tap skip, hold for 2x and picture-in-picture (each optional).
  • Wi-Fi only autoplay now really holds autoplay on mobile data; playing video keeps the screen on, and unplugging headphones pauses.
  • A Video group in Settings: subtitles, audio language, timeline size, quality and where the playback options show.
  • Better contrast, screen-reader labels and large-text layouts across the app.
  • Defaults reviewed: the theme follows the system, unread markers are on, and a one-time tip points to Settings.
  • Comments removed by a moderator are marked Removed in the thread.
  • NSFW: searches follow Show NSFW posts, quarantined communities can be opted into, banned and private ones say why; optional random NSFW community.
  • Errors appear in one tray instead of pop-ups; an image that can never load offers Open in browser.
  • Images linked over http load over https; a link to an image host stays a link.
  • Fixes: GIFs on your own profile autoplay again, lists without a top bar scroll by touch, Hidden tab for local accounts, link decoding on Android 8-12.
  • Many internal checks replaced by behaviour tests, and every land now runs a lint gate.

0.79-beta

8 October 2026 · Release on GitHub

Saved searches, RedGIFs in the app, Reddit favorites sync and a full review of the code.

  • New: saved searches in the Search hub, an optional scroll position indicator, RedGIFs clips in the app (with Show NSFW posts) and favorites synced with Reddit.
  • Reddit emotes, GIFs and images show inline in posts and comments and can be edited; spoilers stay hidden until tapped; escapes, nested styles and lists render right.
  • Safer writing: a post, comment, message or moderator action whose answer was lost is checked with Reddit instead of sent twice, and drafts survive restarts.
  • Accounts stay apart: votes, scroll positions, lists and notifications no longer cross between accounts; a notification for another account switches to it.
  • Fewer crashes and stalls: the app returns where you were after Android closes it, large photos no longer crash previews, and slow work left the main thread.
  • Accessibility: switches, choices and buttons announce what they are and their state, touch targets are larger, and auto-hiding controls follow the system timeout.
  • Correctness: votes, refreshes, paging, search results, markdown, flair and moderator tools now do what they show, and Retry picks up where a load failed.
  • No lost work: drafts, replies and moderator actions survive Android closing the app or a missing answer from Reddit, and backups read back everything they hold.
  • Privacy and security: names and ids stay out of release logs, turning a protection off needs the device unlock, and comment images load only from known image hosts.
  • Speed: lists, settings, backups and links read their data off the main thread, caches are capped, and long threads load more replies on their own.
  • Also: inbox notifications are on by default, shares and links open in the Subshell you already have, numbers follow your language and word filters match whole words.
  • Removed: video downloads. Videos stream; anything an older version downloaded can be deleted in Settings.

0.78.0-beta.1

27 September 2026 · Release on GitHub

App lock, private user notes, sharing into Subshell and content filters everywhere.

  • Optional app lock: unlock with biometrics or the screen lock, choose when it locks again, and optionally hide what notifications say.
  • Private notes and coloured tags on Reddit users, shown next to their name, with an opt-in to include them in backups.
  • Share links, text and images from other apps into Subshell to start a post (optional, off by default).
  • Content filters now work on community pages, custom feeds and search, can be limited to one community, and can show how many posts they hide.
  • Many more Reddit links open inside the app: share links, images and videos, profile tabs, searches, custom feeds and wiki pages.
  • Also new: find in thread, jump between top-level comments, in-app text size, pure black theme, launcher shortcuts, gallery downloads, Giphy GIFs and post flair emoji.

0.77.0-beta.7

Seventh public beta of Subshell.
